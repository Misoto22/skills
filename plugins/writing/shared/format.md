# Format

Read this before laying out any message this plugin produces.

## Match the channel

| Channel | Shape |
|---|---|
| Instant messaging (Slack, Teams, and similar) | One to three lines. No greeting ritual, no sign-off. The request goes in the first line, not the last. |
| Email | A subject line stating the matter and its state, short paragraphs, and one explicit request as the final line. |
| Anything that will be read again months later | Dates, states, and options only. No adjective describing the recipient. |

A subject line names the thing and its status: `PR #482 — release blocked since 28 Jul`, not `Quick question`.

## Match the language

Reply in the language the sender wrote in. Chinese in, Chinese out; English in, English out. Mixed input keeps the mix — do not normalise a bilingual sender into one language.

## Structure earns its place

- Use a list only when the items are genuinely parallel and there are at least three. Two items are a sentence with "and".
- Use a table only when there are at least two columns carrying real data. A two-column table with one row is a sentence.
- One idea per paragraph. A paragraph past five lines is two paragraphs.
- Do not hard-wrap prose. Let the client wrap it.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| We need to decide on the vendor, and the timeline needs confirming too, and I also need to know the budget before I can start. | Three things need deciding before I can start: vendor, timeline, budget. |

## The final line

End with the request, stated as a question or as an action with a date. A message whose last line is "let me know your thoughts" contains no request.
