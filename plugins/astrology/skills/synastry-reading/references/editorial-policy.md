# Editorial policy: editorial-v1

Use this policy as a writing heuristic. It is not a validated compatibility scale, a scoring model, or a substitute for evidence.

## Evidence priority

Prioritize evidence in this order:

1. Confirmed tight aspects involving personal bodies or angles.
2. Repeated mechanisms supported by independent evidence IDs.
3. Exact directional overlays, preserving who activates whose house.
4. Outer planets, nodes, asteroids, Lots, and minor aspects as support.

Treat a lower orb as more prominent only among otherwise comparable evidence. Preserve the configured orb families and exact ledger measurements; do not create a new threshold or compatibility weight.

## Independent support

Count support as independent only when distinct evidence IDs add distinct measured relationships. Do not count any of these as repetition:

- the same aspect restated with reversed prose
- a duplicate or alias of the same geometry
- the same overlay generalized into several life topics
- several conclusions derived from one evidence token

Two aspects with different body pairs can independently repeat a mechanism. An aspect and a directional overlay can reinforce one another when each is relevant, but that combination remains a writing rationale rather than a score.

## Paragraph contract

Write every substantive interpretation as one or more conditional claims with one or more inline evidence IDs from the ledger. Do not rely on an Evidence index entry or a citation in another paragraph to support it.

Use the exact ledger citation before or within the section when recording measurements. Preserve:

- source and target subject IDs
- source and target bodies
- aspect kind or overlay direction
- `exact`, `confirmed`, or `possible` certainty
- exact orb, complete orb range, or target house

Use `can`, `could`, `may`, `might`, `tends to`, `often`, `suggests`, or an equivalent conditional expression in the report language. Separate source facts from interpretation; evidence validation proves artifact integrity, not an astrological conclusion.

## Uncertainty wording

Use these meanings exactly:

- `exact`: the aspect was calculated from two exact instants and carries one recorded orb.
- `confirmed`: every sampled pairing across the declared uncertainty interval stayed within the configured aspect orb; quote the complete range.
- `possible`: at least one sampled pairing entered the configured orb but the contact was not invariant; quote the complete range.

Never turn a range into a midpoint or single orb. Never call `confirmed` scientifically proven, inevitable, or certain in lived experience. Treat missing exact-only houses, angles, derived values, and overlays as declared precision limits rather than measurements to estimate.

## Conditional domains

Authorize romance, friendship, family, work, or money modules only from an explicit request or explicit relationship context. Never infer the domain from labels, pronouns, aspects, overlays, or interpretive tradition.

Keep an explicitly requested module when evidence is weak. State the evidence limit plainly, cite any relevant available evidence, and omit advice presented as chart-supported. Omit every unrequested module, regardless of apparent chart strength.

## Non-predictive boundaries

Do not:

- predict events, duration, fidelity, separation, or outcomes
- diagnose personality, trauma, attachment, mental health, or intent
- assign a percentage, rating, ranking, verdict, or compatibility score
- present medical, legal, financial, or psychological guidance as astrology
- claim that evidence proves a relationship role or guarantees behavior

Offer practical reflection only as optional, conditional experimentation grounded in the cited mechanism. Make clear that astrology is an interpretive tradition rather than a measured causal model.
