# Zi Wei reader report structure

Translate these headings semantically into the user's language. Keep the order.
Everything machine-facing belongs in the separate evidence artifact, not here.

## 1. Life palace and main pattern

Open with how this chart tends to show up in a life, in plain language. Name the
life palace, the stars sitting in it and their brightness, and the palace
opposite it, because an empty or thinly tenanted life palace is read through its
opposite. Say once that this is a traditional model's reading, not a
measurement — then stop apologising and write the interpretation.

## 2. Palace emphases

Develop the two or three palaces the chart actually concentrates on: where the
main stars cluster, which palace the body palace falls in, and what the
three-harmony group around the life palace adds. A compact twelve-palace table
helps here when the reader asked to see the chart; keep it to palace, stem-branch,
and stars.

Do not walk all twelve palaces in order. A list of every palace with one
sentence each is a table, not a reading.

## 3. Transformations, strengths, and tensions

Take the four year transformations as the chart's emphasis. For each, say which
palace it lands in and what that concentration tends to look like day to day.
Give 化忌 its real weight — where attention and friction gather — without turning
its palace into a verdict.

Then give the reader both sides: what this configuration supports, and the
countervailing condition that limits it. Every conclusion needs an everyday
expression and something the reader could actually observe.

If the release did not place a transformation's target star, say so here as a
gap in the calculation. Never let it read as an absent transformation.

## 4. Decade structure and reflection

Describe the decade windows as structural emphases tied to the palaces they
occupy — not as forecasts, and never with attached events or dates. Say once
that this release computes no annual or monthly layer.

Close with two or three low-risk reflection prompts: things the reader can watch
for, not instructions to act on.

## 5. Model data card

A small card, late, so the numbers stay legible without becoming the story:

- bureau, life palace, body palace, and the lunar date the placement used;
- the four transformations and their palaces;
- one sentence stating that star brightness and the transformation set are
  declared lineage conventions, and that another lineage would place some of
  them differently.

End with a single link to the evidence artifact. Nothing machine-facing appears
before that line.

## When an alternate exists

Add a short block before section 5. Keep the three groups separate: what both
charts support, what the boundary changes, and what should be withheld because
of it. Never average the two charts.
