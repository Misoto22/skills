# BaZi compatibility reading template

Follow `shared/report-presentation.md`. Write the reader report and its separate evidence artifact; the headings below are semantic examples, not fixed labels.

| Reader section | Chinese example | English example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 你们的关系画像 | Your relationship pattern |
| 2 | 为什么会互相靠近 | What draws you together |
| 3 | 最容易错位的地方 | Where you may misalign |
| 4 | 双方可能的体验 | Each person's likely experience |
| 5 | 现在值得观察什么 | What to observe now |
| 6 | 模型数据卡 | Model data card |

## Reader report

### 1. 你们的关系画像

- Open with one conditional, plain-language relationship pattern. Develop it in two or three paragraphs: how the pair may feel when things are going well, what makes the pattern meaningful, and the condition that can turn it into friction. Mention an astrological term or score only after the practical meaning is clear.

### 2. 为什么会互相靠近

- Use two or three paragraphs to explain one or two interaction or complementarity findings as everyday sources of ease, interest, or coordination. Show what each person may bring to the exchange; do not promise an outcome.

### 3. 最容易错位的地方

- Use two or three paragraphs to name the most material friction in concrete language: the trigger, how it may look from each side, and what makes the same pattern workable or difficult. Preserve mixed dimensions; a strength and a pressure may coexist.

### 4. 双方可能的体验

- Develop each direction in two or three paragraphs using `Name A → Name B` in the user's language. State what A may offer, what B may receive, and what can make that support miss its mark; never use stored `left` or `right` labels.
- Explain unequal support without blame, rank, dependency, or moral judgment.

### 5. 现在值得观察什么

- Give no more than three observable, reversible questions or conversations, each tied back to the relationship pattern and explained as a real-world check. Use supplied relationship context only; do not infer one from names or demographics.

### 6. 模型数据卡

- When the source selects a relationship context, show its rounded contextual score first and general score second as a reference. Otherwise show the rounded general score and state that no context was selected.
- Show no more than three rounded supporting indicators in the reader report. State once that scores are heuristic model references, not probabilities or relationship verdicts.
- End with one relative link to `bazi_compatibility_evidence_<name-a>_<name-b>.md`.

## Separate evidence artifact

Create `bazi_compatibility_evidence_<name-a>_<name-b>.md`. Include source status, both source-chart checksums, comparison checksum when available, exact five-dimensional weights and values, general and contextual arithmetic, confidence, sensitivity variants, every raw ledger id, and stored ownership/direction. Mirror sections 1–5 as a claim map with the exact source records that support or limit each statement.
