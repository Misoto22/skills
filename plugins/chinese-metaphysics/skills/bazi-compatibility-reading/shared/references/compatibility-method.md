# BaZi compatibility model v1

`bazi-compatibility-v1` is a reproducible heuristic comparison. Its 0-100 values are not probabilities, scientific measurements, or promises about a relationship.

## General index

The general score always uses five dimensions: combined element complementarity 25%, directional day-master support 20%, cross-chart stem and branch interactions 20%, the two day pillars 20%, and structural stability 15%. Every dimension retains its raw inputs, positive and negative adjustments, and final clamp in a ledger.

Element complementarity measures how closely the combined adjusted distributions cover all five elements. Directional support calculates what each person's adjusted distribution supplies to the other person's day element, then averages the two directions without erasing ownership. Interaction scoring starts at 50 and records every cross-chart stem combination, branch combination, clash, harm, and break. Day-pillar scoring keeps stem-element and day-branch evidence separate. Stability starts at 100 and subtracts only declared unresolved-transformation, negative-interaction, and source-confidence penalties.

## Contextual index

Romance, marriage, friendship, family, and work profiles reweight the already-calculated five dimensions into a separate contextual index. They never replace or mutate the general score. When no relationship type is stated, only the relationship-neutral general score is emitted.

## Alternate charts and secondary evidence

When either source has a day-boundary alternate, every primary/alternate combination is compared and reported as a sensitivity range. The primary-primary result remains the displayed general score. Shen Sha is excluded from all numeric dimensions and may appear only as secondary reading context.
