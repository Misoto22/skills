# BaZi Reading

Read one validated chart and write `bazi_reading_<name>.md` without recalculating it.

## Boundary

- Require the complete four pillars, derived facts, scoring ledgers, model versions, confidence, and boundary sensitivity.
- Keep every substantive claim traceable to an exact source fact or score entry.
- Use conditional traditional-interpretation language rather than scientific or deterministic claims.
- Do not calculate Da Yun, annual luck, or specific future events.

The complete source-validation method, report template, evidence order, and worked cases are added with the reading implementation.
