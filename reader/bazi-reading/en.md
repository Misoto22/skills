`bazi-reading` reads a chart that has already been cast and writes the natal analysis for a reader.

## The length is a constraint, not a target

It aims for 1,400 to 1,900 characters — enough to open up each structure, each tension and each observation you could act on, rather than a summary anybody can follow and nobody can use.

The floor stops it collapsing into "your chart is short on water". The ceiling stops it saying the same thing three ways.

## The natal chart only

Its governing constraint is that it is static: the chart itself, never the luck pillars or annual cycles, and no dated prediction.

## The technical evidence is filed separately

Scores, source ids, checksums and the derivation all go to a separate archive file. What the reader receives carries the conclusions and the specific facts behind them, not an output they have to learn to read first.

## What it does not do

It does not cast a chart — that is `bazi-chart`. No compatibility reading, no luck pillars or annual cycles, and no dated prediction.
