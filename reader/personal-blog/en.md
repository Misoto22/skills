`personal-blog` handles a personal post from idea to finished draft, and what it is built around is keeping your voice.

## It extracts the voice before it writes

Rather than applying a generic blog register, it first reads the drafts you already have and extracts the features of your language: the rhythm of long and short sentences, the kinds of comparison you reach for, the words you never use. What it writes afterwards follows that profile.

The reason is that "written well" and "written by you" are two different things, and in a personal post the second one is the content. A passage that reads as entirely correct but is not what you would have said is worth less than a rough true one.

## Every claim can be traced

As it drafts, it marks which statements are cited and which are the author's own judgement, and every factual claim has to trace back to a source. What you review is those two kinds of sentence kept apart, rather than mixed together for you to remember line by line who said what.

## It refuses a few habits of AI writing outright

The throat-clearing opening paragraph, the summarising closing paragraph, and phrases like "in today's rapidly changing landscape". Those three are refused by rule rather than suppressed by prompt.

## What it does not do

It does not write newsletters, magazine profiles, marketing copy, fiction or academic papers, does not edit manuscripts, and does not handle email, chat or repository documentation. Grammar cleanup with nothing at stake is not its job.
