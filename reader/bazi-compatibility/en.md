`bazi-compatibility` compares and scores. The reading is another skill's job.

## Every point traces to a specific relation

It enumerates the interactions between two charts — stem combinations, branch combinations and trines, and the clashes, harms and destructions — writes each into a checkable data file, and scores from that: an overall figure plus separate ones for romance, marriage, friendship, family and work.

The scoring is transparent: every point added or subtracted traces back to the particular stem-and-branch relation behind it, rather than emerging from a black box. A score that cannot be taken apart and checked is no different from an offhand opinion.

## Without the minute it refuses

It does not run on a birth time without minutes. The hour pillar decides the spouse palace, and the spouse palace is the whole subject here — a compatibility score computed from the wrong hour pillar is specific in every digit and wrong in every digit.

## What it does not do

It does not interpret, does not write the report, and produces no verdict on whether two people suit each other. The scores say which areas run smoothly and which need work; `bazi-compatibility-reading` writes them up.
