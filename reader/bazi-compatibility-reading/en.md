`bazi-compatibility-reading` takes compatibility data that has already been computed and writes a 1,800 to 2,400 character report on the relationship. Two constraints shape it.

## It reads in both directions separately

What A brings to B and what B brings to A are two different things, and the report states them separately rather than merging them into one two-way conclusion.

Merging hides the most useful part: the common case in a relationship is that one direction runs easily and the other takes work, and averaged together that reads as "fine overall".

## It stays balanced

Favourable and unfavourable interactions both get written. A report listing only the favourable ones gives the reader nothing to decide with, because it would be true of any two people.

## It refuses a verdict

The data can say which areas run smoothly and which need work. It cannot decide for two people whether to be together.

## What it does not do

It does not compute — that is `bazi-compatibility`. It does not cast a single-person chart, does not touch luck pillars or annual cycles, and makes no dated prediction.
