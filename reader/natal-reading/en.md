`natal-reading` takes a chart that has already been computed and writes it up for a person, keeping the machine detail in a separate audit file.

## Where it starts

The reading starts from the ascendant and its classical ruler, the condition of the sect light, and the few tightest aspects. Those three set the frame of the chart, and everything else is placed against that frame rather than listed on its own.

## Orb carries weight

A square at 0.5° and a square at 7° are not the same sentence. Treating them alike asserts something the chart is not carrying, so the report states each aspect's orb and speaks about a tight one differently from a wide one.

## Boundary degrees are flagged

A body in the first or last degree of a sign is marked as such. A few minutes of error in the birth time moves it into the neighbouring sign, so every conclusion resting on it is conditional — and the report says so rather than carrying on.

## Not computed is not "absent"

A body that could not be computed because ephemeris data was missing is recorded as a gap in the calculation, never written as "you do not have this in your chart". Those two sentences mean entirely different things to a reader, and the second one is false.

## What it does not do

It does not compute a chart from birth details — that is `natal-chart`. No synastry between two people, no transits, progressions or returns, and no dated prediction.
