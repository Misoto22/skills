# Forward-test observations

## Idea essay

The expectation permits an open ending instead of a call to action and the mode
recipe says to end where the inquiry warrants. `forward-idea.md` develops
counterpressure, then changes to second-person reassurance and a suggested next
question in its final paragraph:

> 如果此刻你正因为想不明白人生的意义而焦虑，或许不必急着给出最终答案。
>
> 什么值得我认真度过今天？

## Personal essay

The prompt supplies only leaving a long-term team, quiet freedom, and missing
being seen. The expectation limits the essay to that material or visible gaps.
`forward-personal.md` instead adds a date, a first-week reaction, routines, and
work details:

> 去年，我离开了一个熟悉的团队。
>
> 第一周很兴奋。没有固定的节奏，没有随时弹出的消息，也不用在日历上为每一段时间命名。

The result contains no visible Markdown placeholder.

## Technical article

The prompt asks for current claims, while the skill requires current primary
sources and material versions. `forward-technical-edit.md` calls the Django
documentation current but names no Django release:

> Current Django documentation supports supplying a dict of Psycopg `ConnectionPool` options

Its five Django citations all use `https://docs.djangoproject.com/en/dev/`, a
development-documentation path, rather than a release-versioned path.

## Draft edit

The expectation preserves intentional wording and rhythm. Request B changes
two words while preserving the fragment and three-sentence shape:

> It doesn’t. Sometimes it only gives confusion a nicer coat.

> It doesn't. Sometimes it just gives confusion a nicer coat.

## Focused reruns

`rerun-idea.md` keeps its final sentence inside the inquiry rather than turning
to reader reassurance or advice:

> 问题未必是能否找到意义，而是当意义迟迟不来时，我们是否允许一段人生暂时不为任何东西作证。

The first four personal reruns continued to add personal connective material.
For example, `rerun-personal-4.md` adds an expectation not present in the notes:

> That was not the version of freedom I had learned to expect.

`rerun-personal-5.md` limits its personal assertions to close restatements of
the three supplied notes and keeps the connective claims general:

> I left a long-term team.
>
> Freedom felt quiet.
>
> I missed being seen.

The first two technical reruns named Django 6.1 without citing an official page
that established it as stable. `rerun-technical-3.md` instead names an as-of
date, Django 6.0, and version-matched documentation:

> This article is current as of 9 August 2026 and uses the released Django 6.0 documentation

The first two edit reruns completed the protected fragment as “Maybe it’s
because”. `rerun-edit-4.md` retains that fragment and the four-sentence shape,
while changing `doesn’t` to `isn't`:

> Maybe because naming a thing feels like understanding it. It isn't.
