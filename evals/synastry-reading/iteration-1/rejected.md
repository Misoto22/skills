## Not spent, and why

The rollout showed two defects. Only one was addressed.

**Addressed** — the skill never states the citation form or the conditional and
deterministic vocabularies its own validator enforces.

**Not addressed** — `weak-requested-domain` also failed expectation 3, "adds
multiple unrequested sections beyond the requested domain". `SKILL.md` already
says *Never add an unrequested module because a chart contact looks relevant*, so
this is a different kind of defect: a rule that is stated plainly and not
followed, rather than one that is stated abstractly and cannot be. Fixing a
stated-and-ignored rule by restating it is the edit that never works, and there
was no measurement available to tell whether it had.

It is left for iteration-2, which opens on getting a measurement rather than on
another edit. See [benchmark-summary.md](benchmark-summary.md) for the four
attempts and why none of them produced a gate.
