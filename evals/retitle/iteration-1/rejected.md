# Rejected edits — iteration 1

Two candidates dropped, both on a static reading of the suite.

## Keep "every rename is proposed as a two-column table before a single title is written"

- **Aimed at** — landing inside the audit's 100-150 character range. Cutting the
  whole sentence removes 170; keeping this clause and cutting only the
  creation-time and nine-types clauses removes 88; a shortened form of it lands
  at about -115.
- **Dropped because** — the clause is neither of the two jobs a pointer does. It
  does not say what the material is and it is not a branch anything triggers on:
  no prompt routes to `retitle` because it previews. `SKILL.md` states it as a
  hard rule with the reason attached ("Applying without showing this table is the
  one thing this skill must not do"), and that is the single source. Keeping a
  sentence in the always-loaded field to hit a character target is the target
  driving the edit rather than the finding.
- **Do not re-propose as** — "the preview promise is what makes the skill safe to
  invoke." It is what makes it safe to *run*, which is the body's job.

## Collapse `统一对话命名` and `整理会话标题` into `规范对话名称`

- **Aimed at** — the audit's "one trigger per branch". Three of the five Chinese
  phrases name normalising a title.
- **Dropped because** — the held-out case is `把 Codex 里所有会话标题统一改成带日期的格式`,
  and `统一` and `会话标题` are the two phrases it lands on. An edit that removed
  them would be aimed at a held-out case in the direction that breaks it. The
  whole edit that shipped touches no trigger at all, in either language, which is
  what makes this iteration's gate readable.
- **Do not re-propose as** — "three ways of saying normalise is two too many."
  Count the cases before counting the phrases.
